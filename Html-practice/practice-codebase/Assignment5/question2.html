Introduction

Manali is a beautiful hill station nestled in the mountains of the Indian state of Himachal Pradesh.

Places to Visit

Rohtang Pass, Solang Valley, and Hadimba Temple are must-visit locations.

Food

Enjoy local Himachali cuisine, warm momos, and hot thukpa in the cold weather.

Why I Like It

The scenic beauty and adventurous activities like paragliding make it my favorite destination.
