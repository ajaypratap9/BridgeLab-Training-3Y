Linear Gradient

This section's background is a linear-gradient(). The color transitions in a straight line from one corner/angle to another — here from a deep blue at the top to purple at the bottom.

Radial Gradient with Fixed Attachment

This section's background is a radial-gradient(). The colors radiate outward from a central point like ripples. Because background-attachment: fixed is applied, the background stays still while the page scrolls — creating a subtle parallax-like depth effect as you move up and down.
