Borders and Padding

This page demonstrates four different border styles, rounded corners with border-radius, and different padding values so the internal spacing difference is visually obvious.

Solid Border

border: 4px solid

Small padding - content sits close to the edge.

Dashed Border

border: 4px dashed

Medium padding - a little more breathing room.

Double Border

border: 4px double

Large padding - lots of space around the content.

Groove Border

border: 6px groove

Extra-large padding with a rounded corner look.
